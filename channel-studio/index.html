Channel Studio

A personal tool for producing and publishing short-form video to my own YouTube channels.

What it does

Channel Studio is a command-line application I built and run on my own computer. It turns a written topic into a finished short-form video and publishes it to a YouTube channel I own. It is not a hosted service, has no other users, and no one else can sign in to it.

The pipeline does the following:

  • Generates narration, scene descriptions and title text for a video
  • Creates the artwork and synthesises the voiceover
  • Renders the finished video with captions and a music bed using ffmpeg
  • Runs automated quality checks on the result
  • Uploads the video to my YouTube channel and posts the accompanying comment
  • Reads back that channel's own performance data to guide what I make next

Why it needs YouTube access

The last two steps use the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API. Access is limited to the single channel that authorises the app, and the app only ever acts on that channel's own videos.

Privacy

The app stores its credentials and data locally on my machine and shares nothing with anyone. Full details are in the privacy policy.
